# Additional training compute directory

**Official-source research checked: 24 October 2026.**

This expansion records **142 distinct research entries**: **130 compute offerings/access platforms** plus **12 allocation, legacy or paused-program entries**. It goes beyond the app’s original 13 entries. It is a broad discovery directory, not an exhaustive market census or a list of implemented connectors.

A row is a product/access path, not necessarily an independent company or hardware fleet. Google Cloud expands the existing Google relationship; DigitalOcean GPU Droplets share the existing Paperspace parent. ModelScope/Alibaba, Koyeb/Mistral, Brev/Lepton, research portals and their underlying clouds overlap. Rebrands and repeated products are merged where established.

Compare/filter the [CSV directory](research/additional-provider-directory-2026-10-24.csv). It records training type, accelerator, cost class, eligibility limits, interface, parent grouping, official URLs, research date and **researched-not-implemented** status. The [original-provider audit](PROVIDER_RESEARCH.md) covers Kaggle, Colab, Modal, Lightning and the other existing entries.

## Rebrands, overlaps and exclusions

- **DataCrunch → Verda; Ori → Radiant:** each continuing service is one entry.
- **Voltage Park → Lightning AI:** an existing-vendor alias after their completed merger; not another provider counted here.
- **NVIDIA Brev and DGX Cloud Lepton:** two access products under NVIDIA; partner fleets overlap other clouds.
- **Koyeb / Mistral Compute** share the announced Mistral relationship. **ModelScope / Alibaba** and **DigitalOcean / Paperspace** are also related products.
- **ACCESS, NAIRR, CloudBank, Jetstream2/Delta, TRC and other allocations:** overlapping funding/host routes, not additive guaranteed GPU inventories.
- **Coiled:** orchestration allowance does not pay the cloud GPU bill. Free Ray, SkyPilot, BinderHub, Runhouse/Kubetorch and similar software do not supply their own free GPUs.
- **Inference APIs/assistant credits:** excluded as training-compute evidence unless current official documentation establishes actual training. Hugging Face ZeroGPU demo time is not a general unattended training quota.
- **Unqualified leads:** Atlas Cloud, Fluidstack retail access, Cerebrium training, Serverspace training configurations, HUMAIN, stc, Sahm, Macquarie and CloudSigma remain follow-up leads with reasons in the reports. Future datacenter announcements and hardware sales do not count as currently bookable training resources.

## Recommended MultiGPU priorities

This ordering is an engineering inference from published interfaces and the existing app architecture, not measured performance, a price ranking or live connector certification.

1. **Free/introductory notebook qualification:** Saturn and ModelScope, then account-qualified regional options. Test entitlement, checkpoints and local export before automation.
2. **Small credited job integrations:** machine.dev, Akash and eligible AMD Developer Cloud. Short job lifetime, legal/payment conditions and ROCm compatibility need explicit handling.
3. **Reusable paid GPU worker:** start with Runpod, Lambda, Hyperstack, Vast.ai, Beam or Verda. Use a shared container/SSH worker with provider-specific provisioning, billing and termination adapters.
4. **Regional access:** investigate E2E, AceCloud, Yotta, Jarvislabs and Neysa for India; Sakura/TWAI and the other regional clouds for appropriately eligible users.
5. **Managed training:** a separate recipe/API family for Tinker, Together, Fireworks, Replicate and fal. Verify portable model/adapter output; do not present these as arbitrary-script GPUs.
6. **Institutional connector:** authorized SSH/Slurm/OpenStack execution after the user has an approved allocation; match that actual host rather than treating funding portals as worker pools.

Before a new connector is supported, test launch, actual device, five-minute reporting, account allowance/expiry, all billable extras, cancellation, interruption recovery, credentials expiry, laptop disconnection, incomplete checkpoints, insufficient local disk and a usable verified local model. Preserve the requested training target and require explicit paid usage. CUDA, ROCm, TPU/XLA, Gaudi and Trainium require different runtime qualification.
