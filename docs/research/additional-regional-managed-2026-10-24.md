## IndiaAI: a funding route rather than another cloud fleet

The **IndiaAI Compute Portal** offers access to empanelled suppliers through eligibility, verification and applicable subsidy/approval rules. This is **conditional subsidized compute**, not universally free GPUs. Users may owe uncovered costs. Its supplier roster overlaps E2E, Neysa, Sify, Yotta, NxtGen and other commercial vendors; do not count awarded resources again as a second pool. [Government portal](https://www.compute.indiaai.gov.in/login), [end-user policy](https://compute.indiaai.gov.in/files/end-user-policy.pdf), [cost calculator](https://compute.indiaai.gov.in/indiaaipricecalculator).

## Additional scientific access

**UseGalaxy.eu** is a free public scientific analysis service, with an official GPU-enabled Jupyter tool and protein-model fine-tuning tutorials. This is a **domain-focused, interactive research candidate**; it does not establish permission or capacity for unrestricted unattended training. Its actual tool availability, queues, quotas and model export must be tested. Other Galaxy hosts do not automatically offer the same GPU tools. [Official hosted-service directory](https://galaxyproject.org/use/usegalaxy-eu/), [GPU training tool and tutorials](https://training.galaxyproject.org/training-material/by-tool/interactive_tool_ml_jupyter_notebook.html).

**Chameleon** provides allocation-funded GPU VMs/bare metal for approved open academic computer-science research. Current policy emphasizes eligible US researchers and international collaborators; commercial goals are excluded. Projects receive service units rather than cash/GPU-hours, and GPU leases have a seven-day maximum subject to reservations. This is a **research allocation route**, not a general free training cloud. [Current eligibility, allocations and GPU limits](https://www.chameleoncloud.org/learn/frequently-asked-questions/).

## Investigated but not qualified as an additional training provider

**Cerebrium** currently documents a serverless platform centered on real-time inference. Custom GPU containers alone do not establish supported long training lifetimes, durable recovery or training-specific billing. Keep it as a follow-up lead until a current training execution contract is verified. [Current product documentation](https://cerebrium.ai/docs/getting-started/introduction).

**ClearML** and **Outerbounds** can manage training infrastructure, but their orchestration/control-plane features do not establish an additional free compute allocation. Outerbounds' current site also announces it is joining Anaconda. Keep software/platform licensing separate from the user's actual cloud resources. [ClearML pricing](https://clear.ml/pricing), [Outerbounds](https://outerbounds.com/).

Inference API credits, AI assistant balances, free control planes and free orchestration software were excluded from free-GPU totals. Account tests must establish actual training access, permitted use, progress reporting, checkpoint survival, cancellation, all billable extras and a usable local model before MultiGPU advertises support.
