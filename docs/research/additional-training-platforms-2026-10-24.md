# Additional China and specialist training platforms

Checked **24 October 2026** using official provider resources only. **Nine additional platforms** have an explicit training path in official product or execution documentation. This establishes research relevance, not a supported MultiGPU connector or a live execution/billing test. No new unconditional free GPU allocation was established in this extension.

## Independent review of the India free-credit claims

**AceCloud:** its current FAQ explicitly states ₹20,000 credit for 30 days, no credit card to claim, usable across GPU instances, compute, storage and other products. The same source documents PyTorch/TensorFlow images and training clusters. This is **GPU-capable trial credit, not inference-only credit**. Actual account eligibility, GPU stock and exhaustion controls still require testing. [Official FAQ](https://acecloud.ai/faqs/).

**Yotta Shakti Studio:** its current offer explicitly permits fine-tuning jobs and H100/L40S serverless GPU minutes in addition to tokens. It states ₹10,000 credit, 30-day validity, and no automatic charges after exhaustion. The longer claim process requires business email, claim form, business KYC and a representative call, despite shorter instant-activation language elsewhere on the same page. Treat it as a **verified-account promotional training grant**, with the award's start date and limits read from the account. [Official offer and eligibility FAQ](https://shakticloud.ai/shakti-studio-free-ai-cloud-credits/).

The broader regional report's distinction between inference APIs, managed training, paid GPU infrastructure and research allocations appears sound. No source review here proves provider-side zero-spend enforcement.
