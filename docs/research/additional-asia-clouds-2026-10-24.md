The extension includes New Zealand and Canada as well as Asian providers. This is a published-offer review; none is an implemented or account-qualified MultiGPU connector. Multiple product names under each vendor count once.

## Restricted academic route: Cybera Rapid Access Cloud

Cybera's current program page provides free cloud resources to **Alberta-based academics, researchers, students and classrooms within Cybera member institutions**. It describes CPU/RAM allocations and flexible project duration. This can support approved CPU training or data preparation, but **no GPU entitlement was established**. It belongs under an institutional/research category, not the commercial vendor count or public free GPU pool. Older PDFs describe broader eligibility; apply the current program page's membership restriction. [Current program and eligibility](https://www.cybera.ca/rapid-access-cloud/), [Current cloud services overview](https://www.cybera.ca/cloud/).

## Integration consequences

These vendors broaden geographic coverage without creating 14 guaranteed free pools. **Sakura DOK** and **TWAI Cloud** have concrete published introductory benefits worth account-level investigation. The remaining twelve commercial vendors are paid candidates unless a separate eligible grant or contract is observed. Cybera is a restricted academic CPU route and adds no guaranteed GPU pool.

Start with a generic authorized SSH/container/Slurm interface where the provider permits it. VM/task provisioning, interruption, billing shutdown, checkpoints, five-minute progress and verified local model delivery require separate connector qualification. Keep provider-specific data residency, member verification, regional availability and third-party-access terms visible. No account was created, training job launched or payment method activated during this research.

The extension is frozen at 15 records. LG Uplus, TrueIDC and separate Canadian cluster portals were not added without a sufficiently clear current training-product/eligibility qualification in this pass. Their omission does not establish absence of a suitable product. Planned new facilities and multiple portals for the same research allocation were not counted as extra available capacity.
