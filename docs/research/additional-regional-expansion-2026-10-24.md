# Regional GPU training expansion

Official-source research checked **24 October 2026**. This adds **15 paid compute offerings** to the earlier directory: **8 GPU-cloud offerings** and **7 enterprise/sales-qualified offerings**. It does not establish 15 independent hardware fleets or implemented connectors.

All entries provide documented GPU infrastructure or a currently marketed training service. No account was opened, GPU rented or training run performed. Regional signup eligibility, identity/KYC checks, accepted payment instruments, contracting entity and access from the user's location remain **unverified**, except where a source-specific restriction is stated below. General cloud trials and grants are not assumed to cover GPU charges.

## Leads kept out of the qualified count

- **Serverspace:** paid NVIDIA A16 vGPU instances are confirmed. However, its current page emphasizes 4–16 GB A16 inference/VDI and future RTX Pro 6000 availability, while a lower comparison advertises A100/H100 training. Hardware/profile/CUDA licensing and real bookable training configurations need verification before adding a training provider. [Official product](https://serverspace.us/services/gpu-cloud-server/). Serverspace identifies ITGLOBAL.COM as its operator; do not count both brands as independent suppliers without reviewing the product path.
- **HUMAIN:** official pages found in this sweep establish the company, a marketplace and model-preview APIs, but not an accessible current arbitrary GPU training rental interface. Large future deployments are not capacity users can book. [Company launch](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/news-and-insights/press-releases/2025/hrh-crown-prince-launches-humain-as-global-ai-powerhouse/), [marketplace](https://marketplace.humain.com/), [Node model preview](https://node.humain.com/).
- **solutions by stc:** its annual report confirms GPUaaS launched, so it is a real enterprise lead. A current customer training environment, hardware configuration, pricing and launch interface were not established here. Keep stc/solutions under one parent rather than counting separate names. [Official 2025 report](https://solutions.com.sa/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/solutions-by-stc-AR25_English_Full_compressed.pdf).
- **Sahm Cloud:** its site returned a verification challenge; no current official training offering was verified. [Official site](https://sahmcloud.com/).
- **Macquarie Cloud Services:** current Launch AI is a managed sovereign AI product; the inspected page does not establish arbitrary GPU training execution. CAUDIT research-compute material is a promising institution-qualified lead, but exact GPU access and training interface require follow-up. [Launch AI](https://www.macquariecloudservices.com/data-ai/launch-ai/), [research-compute discussion](https://www.macquariecloudservices.com/blog/the-new-realities-of-research-compute-hpc-ai-and-the-infrastructure-shift-ahead/).

## MultiGPU implementation order

The strongest reusable routes in this group are GPU VM adapters for **Yandex Cloud, Selectel, Servercore and HOSTKEY**, plus a notebook/job adapter for **Cloud.ru**. This is an engineering inference from the documented execution interfaces. Confirm the user's contracting/payment eligibility first, then test launch, actual GPU, five-minute reporting, cancellation, checkpoint recovery and verified local export. Core42 is a promising Middle East option with access vetting; the Australian services fit enterprise/research procurement better than beginner free-only onboarding.

Keep these as **researched, not implemented** entries. No general free-only job should launch solely because a marketing page advertises a trial, migration grant or freely downloadable image.
