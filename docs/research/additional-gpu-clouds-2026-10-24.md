# Additional paid GPU clouds and access platforms

Research checked against official websites and documentation on **24 October 2026**. This is discovery research for MultiGPU, not a claim that these services are integrated or that account capacity has been tested.

## Scope and counting

This report identifies **40 additional paid compute offerings** with official evidence of rented GPUs or executable training infrastructure, plus **4 GPU access platforms**. The 40 include both self-service clouds/marketplaces and enterprise reserved infrastructure. They are not 40 guaranteed independent hardware fleets. DigitalOcean GPU Droplets, for example, are a separate product from the app's existing Paperspace notebooks under the same company. Marketplaces may source capacity from clouds listed elsewhere.

The four access platforms are NVIDIA Brev, NVIDIA DGX Cloud Lepton, Prime Intellect, and Shadeform. Brev and Lepton are both NVIDIA products, and the latter three aggregate other providers. Do not add their advertised GPU fleets to supplier fleet counts.

No universal recurring free GPU entitlement was established for the 40 paid offerings below. This means **paid by default**, not that every possible grant, promotion, academic allocation or negotiated credit has been disproved. Two official conditional credit routes are recorded separately. Free software, no subscription fee, no egress fee, a free Kubernetes control plane, and a zero-priced account are not free GPU compute.

## Conditional credits found during this paid-cloud sweep

- **Denvr AI Ascend:** the official page invites early-stage applicants for an initial **$1,000** startup credit and says eligible teams may receive up to **$500,000** in production credits. These are assessed startup-program awards, not an automatic credit on every personal signup. Amount, expiry, covered GPUs and any matching-spend terms need an award/account check. [Official program description](https://www.denvr.com/).
- **Radiant AI Cloud:** its official support FAQ says free trial credits can be offered after the user contacts support with a use case. No amount or recurring schedule is specified there. Record as **approval-based trial**, with allowance unknown until an award is observed. [Official trial FAQ](https://docs.ori.co/support/faq/).
- **Oblivus:** official pricing says it has no formal student/research credit program, although occasional projects/events can receive credits by request. Treat as a contact route, never a general free tier. [Official FAQ](https://oblivus.com/pricing/).

## Best next integration candidates

This ordering is an engineering inference from the documented interfaces, not measured performance or a commercial endorsement.

1. **Runpod Pods:** documented GPU lifecycle and persistent storage, broad hardware catalog and a container worker model. Pin a supported API version; current v2 beta needs compatibility monitoring.
2. **Lambda Cloud:** straightforward GPU VM/API workflow and clear terminate-to-stop billing semantics. Start with single-node training.
3. **Hyperstack:** documented infrastructure lifecycle and account-specific pricebook make both resource selection and cost preview tractable. Validate deletion/hibernation and persistent extras.
4. **Vast.ai:** broad low-cost rental marketplace and an official Python CLI/SDK. Require host-quality filters, immutable offer selection, restart recovery and storage-cost previews.
5. **Beam:** documented long-running Python training and persistent checkpoint volumes make it a natural alternative to Modal's compute API path.
6. **Verda / DigitalOcean GPU Droplets / Scaleway:** strong VM/cluster candidates for paid-account users. Validate each provider's stop-versus-delete and storage semantics separately.
7. **Jarvislabs / Thunder Compute:** focused developer GPU products with SSH/Jupyter/VM workflows; public programmatic lifecycle completeness is the next qualification step.
8. **Shadeform / Prime Intellect / Brev:** useful breadth through fewer integrations, but they add a platform billing layer or provider-placement choices. Never silently route to a different paid provider.

For the current MultiGPU architecture, **GPU VM or container execution is more reusable than provider-specific browser notebook automation**. A shared SSH/container worker can cover multiple paid clouds once each has a thin authenticated provisioning adapter. Free-only launch should still stay disabled unless an account's actual applicable credits and enforceable spending controls are verified. A local training timer cannot stop retained-disk, IP, network, platform or reserved-capacity charges.

## Qualification required before a connector is supported

For each candidate, verify with an authorized account: credential scope/expiry; current GPU and region availability; launch idempotency; exact hardware and driver; logs and five-minute progress; checkpoint persistence; stop/cancel/delete behavior; interruption recovery; disk and transfer charges; account price/credit expiry; and a verified usable local export. Marketing evidence in this report does not satisfy those acceptance checks.
