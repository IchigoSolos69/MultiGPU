# Additional free, promotional and credit-backed training compute

Research date: **24 October 2026**. Official provider resources only. These are additions to the app's existing 13-provider directory, not implemented or account-qualified connectors. No accounts were created and no paid resources were launched. Provider advertisements are evidence of an offer, not evidence of remaining capacity or an enforceable zero-spend limit.

The strongest additional candidates to investigate are **Saturn Cloud, ModelScope Notebook, AMD Developer Cloud, machine.dev, Akash, Alibaba PAI-DSW, OVHcloud and Thunder Compute's student program**. Their free access models differ substantially. The following findings distinguish ongoing allowances, one-time grants, trials, paid activation and retired onboarding.

## Integration priorities inferred from the evidence

1. Qualify **Saturn and ModelScope assisted notebook** workflows, checking real allocation, entitlement, runtime lifetime, file export and PyTorch compatibility first.
2. Prototype **machine.dev and Akash** job connectors in isolated accounts with explicit grant limits; they have small grants plus documented automated execution. Disable purchase/top-up/fallback implicitly.
3. Add **AMD ROCm** as a separate recipe/backend qualification. An MI300X is not a CUDA GPU; existing CUDA requirements must not silently be reused.
4. Add **PAI-DSW and OVHcloud** as trial/paid connectors with account-level credit coverage and stop controls. Exclude automatic free-only until zero-spend conditions are enforceable.
5. Treat **Tencent, Baidu and Huawei** as region/account-qualified assisted candidates, with guided steps and clear conditional badges.
6. Keep GPU demo APIs, free SDKs, free control planes, paid subscription benefits, grants requiring applications and closed new-user services out of the unconditional free GPU category.

These priorities are engineering judgments from documented interfaces and access models, not results from live lifecycle, billing or export tests.
